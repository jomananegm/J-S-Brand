# Research & consumer-psychology notes

## What the strongest brands in the category do
| Brand | What works | What J'S takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| **Crumbl** | Redrew its logo with thicker, more ownable letters. Its pink box and unboxing are part of the product. Ordering is gamified (live box preview, flavour voting). | A custom, bold wordmark. The box itself is the hero, with a 3D box you can open on the site. |
| **Cinnamood** | Sells itself as a "Daily Mood Boost" lifestyle, not a bakery. Leads with a sharing box ("Box of 4"). | "Not a bakery. A mood." Lead with one sharing box. |
| **Cinnabon** | Once ran a one-day site takeover where visitors scraped "frost" off the screen to reveal prizes. | Playful interaction on the page: the *Draw your drizzle* game. |
| **Top bakery sites (2026 roundups)** | Their one job is to make people hungry. Large product imagery. Organising by **occasion** rather than product type is an under-used conversion win. Restraint reads as premium. | Big product visuals, a *What's the mood?* occasion picker, one product in focus. |
| **3D product staging (Awwwards)** | A tight palette, 3D and motion used as the main storytelling tool. | A CSS 3D box in brand colours, with no heavy WebGL libraries, so it stays fast on phones. |

## Psychology applied on the site
1. **All-in price, shown up front: "450 EGP, delivery included."**
   Hidden fees that show up at checkout are seen as sneaky by 86% of shoppers in Which?'s research.
   In StubHub's field test, shoppers who met fees only at checkout were much more likely to drop off than those shown the full price up front.
   J'S shows the full price everywhere: hero, product page, cart and WhatsApp message.
2. **Price per roll: "= 37.5 EGP a roll."**
   A bundle price that divides cleanly shifts attention from the whole box to the single unit, which makes the purchase easier to justify.
3. **Split the box (social proof → social math).**
   The slider turns 450 into "112.5 EGP each" for 4 friends and shows whose rolls are whose.
   It reframes the price around the real buyer, a group, and gives a ready-made reason to share the link in the group chat.
4. **Occasion framing.** *What's the mood?* (study night, girls' night, birthday, office, just because) gives people permission to treat themselves without needing a special day.
   It also carries the mood into the order note, so J'S knows the context.
5. **The unboxing reward.** The lid opens automatically when the section scrolls into view, the way a real unboxing does. It builds anticipation before the price is shown.
6. **Low-effort checkout.** One page, one box, a sticky "Add the box" bar on mobile, the form remembered between visits, and a pre-written WhatsApp message.
7. **Honesty as a brand asset.** No fake scarcity, countdowns or reviews. These only appear when real launch data is entered.
