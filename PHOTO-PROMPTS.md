# Real photos — shot list + AI image prompts

The site uses original 3D renders until real photos exist.
For each shot, either photograph the real product or generate a brand-matched image with your AI image tool.
Then save it in `assets/images/` and set the path in `js/config.js → photos`.

**Don't use Pinterest images on the site.** Other people own them. Use Pinterest only as a mood reference for your own shoot.

**Always:** show the real J'S roll and box. If you use AI images, don't present them as customer photos or reviews.

**Shared style (paste into every prompt):**
> editorial food photography, warm dramatic side light, glossy white icing, visible cinnamon swirl texture, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, colour palette burgundy #5E0B13, blush pink #E1ACB5, vanilla cream #FDF2D2, espresso brown #28110C, retro checkerboard paper, playful Gen Z lifestyle mood, no text, no logos, high detail, 4:5

**Phone shooting tips:** shoot by a window in late-afternoon light. Use a blush or burgundy card as the background and checkerboard paper under the rolls.
Shoot at 4:5 and include one hand for scale and life.
