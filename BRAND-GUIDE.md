# J'S — Brand Guide (v2)

## Positioning
**J'S is not a bakery. It's a mood.**
J'S is an everyday little-treat brand for Gen Z, students and friend groups in Alexandria.
It starts with one hero product, **The Big Mood Box** (12 Classic Cinnamon Rolls, 450 EGP, delivery included), and grows from there.

- **Tagline:** A LITTLE SOMETHING GOOD.
- **Campaign line:** LITTLE TREATS. BIG MOODS.
- **Arabic lines:** مش لازم تكون مناسبة. ممكن تبقى رولز. · الحاجات الصغيرة تستاهل. · قسّمها على الشلة.

### Why "The Big Mood Box"
- It takes the campaign line ("Big moods") and makes it something you can order. Every time someone says the product name, they say the brand idea.
- 12 rolls is a group-size format. The name tells you it's for sharing.
- It leaves room for a smaller sibling later: the **Mini Mood Box**, already listed as "Coming later".

## Logo system (assets/brand/)
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `wordmark-sticker.svg` | Primary. Cream bubble letters, espresso outline and offset shadow. Use on burgundy and espresso. |
| `wordmark-sticker-blush.svg` | Footer and dark backgrounds. |
| `wordmark-burgundy.svg` / `-cream.svg` / `-espresso.svg` | Flat one-colour versions for print, stamps and embroidery. |
| `lockup-burgundy.svg` / `lockup-cream.svg` | Wordmark + "THE LITTLE TREAT BRAND". |
| `seal.svg` | Round sticker seal with circular text, for box lids, bags and corners. |
| `monogram.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `icon-512.png` | Profile picture, favicon, app icon. |

**The idea:** the apostrophe in J'S is a tiny cinnamon roll. The brand's signature product sits inside its name.
The letters are drawn as paths, not typed, so the logo looks the same everywhere.

**Clear space:** keep at least the height of the apostrophe-roll empty around the logo.
**Minimum size:** 28px tall on screen; use the favicon below that.

Rules: text is always clearly dark-on-light or light-on-dark. Blush is never used for long text on cream.
No gradients except for food lighting.

## Type
- **Display:** Lilita One. Headlines in CAPS, tight leading (0.86–1.05).
- **Body / UI:** Poppins 400–700.
- **Packaging print:** Poppins 700 in CAPS with wide letter-spacing.
- **Egyptian Arabic, playful:** Baloo Bhaijaan 2, 700–800. It's a rounded partner to Lilita One and is used for every عامية line next to an English headline.
- **Arabic, poetic:** Amiri Bold, only for الحاجات الصغيرة تستاهل-type lines. **Arabic UI:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- **Bilingual rule:** English headline first, then one Egyptian Arabic line that adds the feeling. See `COPY-DECK.md`.

## Graphic language
Checkerboard liner paper · admit-one tickets with punched sides · round sticker seals · tilted stickers with hard offset shadows · a hand-drawn icing drizzle · the 3D box as the hero object.

## Voice
Short, warm, a little cheeky. Talk like a friend handing over a box, never like a sales pitch.
- ✅ "Twelve rolls go a long way." · "One price. That's it." · "No judgment."
- ❌ "Indulge in our premium artisanal…" · fake urgency · fake reviews

## Packaging (as rendered on the site)
- **Lid outside:** burgundy, sticker wordmark, "THE BIG MOOD BOX / 12 CLASSIC CINNAMON ROLLS", seal in the corner.
- **Lid inside:** blush and cream checkerboard with a "A LITTLE SOMETHING GOOD." label. This is the unboxing moment.
- **Walls:** burgundy with a checkerboard band. Front reads "LITTLE TREATS. BIG MOODS."
- **Liner:** cream checker paper, rolls in a 4 × 3 grid.
